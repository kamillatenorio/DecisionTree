We are zookeepers in a zoo and responsible for feeding the monkeys.


The monkeys look very cute, but we have to be careful, because some of the monkeys bite.

We already know which of the monkeys currently in our zoo bite and which do not.


But new monkeys are coming to our zoo and joining the group of monkeys.


So we need to find a way to figure out which of these new monkeys bite and which do not – preferably without getting too close to their teeth.

We have decided to list which of our current monkeys bite and which do not.

As we can see, the monkeys differ in certain features, such as the shape of their eyes and whether they are smiling or winking.

Our task is to find a way, based on the features of the monkeys, that helps us determine whether new monkeys coming to the zoo will bite or not.

How can we do that?
